Sophia Commons in Claude and ChatGPT

Ask Claude or ChatGPT about anthroposophical life and get real places, dates and links from Sophia Commons: about 4,800 Waldorf schools, biodynamic farms, Camphill communities, clinics and study groups in 79 countries, upcoming events, recorded talks, and an index of 6,800 lectures by Rudolf Steiner with his books and free audio. It's free, read-only and needs no account.

Connect it

Claude

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector. (Once Sophia Commons is listed in Claude's directory, you can add it from there instead.)
  2. Name it Sophia Commons and use this address:https://sophiacommons.org/mcp?src=claude
  3. Leave the sign-in settings empty. In a new chat, turn on Sophia Commons from the tools menu and ask a question.

ChatGPT

  1. On chatgpt.com, open Settings, then Apps & Connectors. Under Advanced settings, turn on Developer mode.
  2. Create a new app named Sophia Commons with this address and no authentication:https://sophiacommons.org/mcp?src=chatgpt
  3. In a new chat, add Sophia Commons from the + menu and ask a question.

Other apps

Any app that supports the Model Context Protocol can connect to https://sophiacommons.org/mcp (Streamable HTTP, no sign-in).

Things to ask

  • Find Waldorf schools near Stuttgart.
  • Are there biodynamic farms within 50 km of Portland, Oregon?
  • What anthroposophical events are coming up in Switzerland this winter?
  • What did Rudolf Steiner say about bees?
  • Which lectures did Steiner give in Dornach in 1924?
  • What did Steiner lecture on today's date?
  • Find recorded talks about the etheric body.
  • Welche Vorträge hielt Steiner über Karma? (Ask in German and the Steiner results link the German texts first.)

Where the app supports it, places appear on a map with cards, and events have an Add to calendar button.

Every tool only reads public information. Nothing is posted, changed or bought. Email addresses and phone numbers are never returned.

Where the information comes from

Places and events are the public listings on Sophia Commons, gathered from public sources and from the organizations themselves. The lecture index holds titles, dates, places and links from the Rudolf Steiner Archive (rsarchive.org); the texts stay there, and every answer links to them. Audio recordings are on rudolfsteineraudio.com. You can search the same lecture index yourself in the Steiner Library.

Privacy

For each search we keep a short record: which tool, whether it came from Claude or ChatGPT, the search words, how many results came back and how long it took. No name, email, account or IP address, and we never see the rest of your conversation. Records are deleted after 18 months. Details are in our privacy policy.
