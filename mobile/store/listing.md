## Full description (both stores)
Sophia Commons is a free directory and events calendar for the anthroposophical community worldwide.

Find what's near you. Search nearly 4,800 Waldorf and Steiner schools, kindergartens, biodynamic farms, Camphill communities, Christian Community congregations, study groups, clinics, eurythmists and initiatives in over 75 countries. Tap "Near me" to see the closest places, then call, email, visit the website or get directions.

See what's happening. The calendar brings together conferences, courses, festivals, lectures and study groups, in person and online. Each event shows the time where you are and the local time where it takes place. Add an event to your calendar, share it, or save it and get a reminder the day before.

Keep the year. The app follows the festivals and anniversaries of the year, from Advent and the Holy Nights to Easter, St. John's and Michaelmas, and shows each week's verse from Rudolf Steiner's Calendar of the Soul.

Listen and read. New talks from anthroposophical YouTube channels, essays from Substack writers, and podcasts, all in one place.

Saved events and places stay on your phone, so they open even without a connection.

No ads. No account needed. Available in English, German, Spanish and Russian.

## Privacy answers
- Data collected by the app: none. There is no account and no tracking.
- Location: used only on the phone when "Near me" is tapped, to sort places by distance. Not stored or sent anywhere.
- Notifications: reminders for saved events are scheduled on the phone itself.
- Saved events and places: stored on the phone only.
- App Store "Data Not Collected": yes. Google Play Data safety: no data collected or shared.
