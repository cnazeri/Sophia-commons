In Memoriam  ·  Honoring those who crossed the Threshold

"The human being is not what he is, but what he is becoming."
- Rudolf Steiner

This space honors members of the anthroposophical community who have crossed the threshold. Search for someone below or browse the memorials. To add a tribute, use the form at the bottom.

Showing 6 memorials

Rudolf Steiner

1861 - 1925
Kraljevec, Austria-Hungary / Dornach, Switzerland

Founder of anthroposophy, Waldorf education, biodynamic agriculture, and anthroposophic medicine. His legacy continues through thousands of initiatives worldwide.

Marie Steiner-von Sivers

1867 - 1948
Wloclawek, Poland / Beatenberg, Switzerland

Co-worker of Rudolf Steiner and guardian of his literary estate. Developed speech formation and dramatic arts as a path of spiritual practice.

Ita Wegman

1876 - 1943
Karawang, Dutch East Indies / Arlesheim, Switzerland

Co-founder of anthroposophic medicine alongside Rudolf Steiner. Established the first anthroposophic clinic in Arlesheim, Switzerland in 1921.

EP

Ehrenfried Pfeiffer

1899 - 1961
Munich, Germany / Spring Valley, NY, USA

Pioneer of biodynamic agriculture in North America. Founded the Threefold Farm and developed soil testing methods used worldwide.

KK

Karl König

1902 - 1966
Vienna, Austria / Brachenreuthe, Germany

Founder of the Camphill movement in 1940, creating inclusive communities for people with developmental disabilities across the world.

Albert Steffen

1884 - 1963
Wynau, Switzerland / Dornach, Switzerland

Swiss poet, playwright, and president of the General Anthroposophical Society from 1925 to 1963. Carried forward Steiner's artistic impulse.

About Sophia Commons

Sophia Commons is a free, community-maintained directory for the anthroposophical world: Waldorf schools, biodynamic farms, Camphill communities, anthroposophic medicine, eurythmy, study groups and more, in one place.

What you'll find

  • The world directory of organizations, searchable by category and region
  • An events calendar with festivals, conferences and courses
  • The Rudolf Steiner library with links to free and printed editions
  • In Memoriam honoring members of the community

How you can help

  • Submit a listing for an organization we're missing (reviewed within 48 hours)
  • Use "Report a problem" on any listing that is out of date
  • Volunteer as an editor

Privacy

Privacy at Sophia Commons

Sophia Commons is a free community directory. We collect as little as we can, never sell data, and use it only to run the site. Last updated September 27, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details if you sign up: your email address and a password (stored securely by our database provider, Supabase). Your username is shown on chat messages and comments you post.
  • What you submit: listings, events, memorial tributes and photos, comments, chat messages and reports. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Usage statistics: pages viewed, a random session id, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language. We use this to see which parts of the site are useful. It does not include your name or email.
  • Google Analytics collects similar anonymous usage statistics. Google Maps loads on the Directory page to show the map.

Stored on your device

Your language choice and, if you sign in, a login token are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: Supabase (database and accounts), Netlify (hosting), Google (Analytics and Maps) and our email provider for the newsletter. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account, your submissions or your newsletter subscription by emailing hello@sophiacommons.org.

Questions? Write to hello@sophiacommons.org.
