Rudolf Steiner himself foresaw the lasting importance of The Philosophy of Freedom. In a 1922 conversation, Walter Johannes Stein asked him:

"What will remain of your work in thousands of years?"

Steiner replied: "Nothing but The Philosophy of Freedom."

Rudolf Steiner

1861 - 1925
Kraljevec, Austria-Hungary / Dornach, Switzerland

Founder of anthroposophy, Waldorf education, biodynamic agriculture, and anthroposophic medicine. His legacy continues through thousands of initiatives worldwide.

Marie Steiner-von Sivers

1867 - 1948
Wloclawek, Poland / Beatenberg, Switzerland

Co-worker of Rudolf Steiner and guardian of his literary estate. Developed speech formation and dramatic arts as a path of spiritual practice.

Ita Wegman

1876 - 1943
Karawang, Dutch East Indies / Arlesheim, Switzerland

Co-founder of anthroposophic medicine alongside Rudolf Steiner. Established the first anthroposophic clinic in Arlesheim, Switzerland in 1921.

EP

Ehrenfried Pfeiffer

1899 - 1961
Munich, Germany / Spring Valley, NY, USA

Pioneer of biodynamic agriculture in North America. Founded the Threefold Farm and developed soil testing methods used worldwide.

Karl König

1902 - 1966
Vienna, Austria / Brachenreuthe, Germany

Founder of the Camphill movement in 1940, creating inclusive communities for people with developmental disabilities across the world.

Albert Steffen

1884 - 1963
Wynau, Switzerland / Dornach, Switzerland

Swiss poet, playwright, and president of the General Anthroposophical Society from 1925 to 1963. Carried forward Steiner's artistic impulse.

Sergei O. Prokofieff

1954 - 2014
Moscow, Russia / Dornach, Switzerland

Russian anthroposophist and author of more than 30 books on esoteric Christianity, beginning with Rudolf Steiner and the Founding of the New Mysteries. Co-founded the Anthroposophical Society in Russia and served on the Goetheanum Executive Council from 2001 to 2013. His books

About Sophia Commons

Sophia Commons is a free, community-maintained directory for the anthroposophical world: Waldorf schools, biodynamic farms, Camphill communities, anthroposophic medicine, eurythmy, study groups and more, in one place.

What's on the site

The directory

More than 4,500 organizations in 77 countries: Waldorf and Steiner schools, kindergartens and teacher trainings, biodynamic and Demeter farms, Camphill communities and curative education, anthroposophic doctors, clinics and therapists, eurythmy schools and ensembles, Christian Community congregations, anthroposophical societies and branches, eldercare, and a new science and technology section. Each listing has its contact details and, where we could find one, a picture. You can browse by category or region, see everything on the world map, or use "Find near me" to sort listings by distance. Open the Directory to start, or try the links to related directories on that page.

Events and news

The Events page brings together news from the movement and a shared calendar of conferences, courses, study groups, webinars and the festivals of the year. Every event can be added to your own Google, Outlook, Microsoft 365 or Apple calendar with one click. Members of the School of Spiritual Science will find First Class gatherings under Blue Card Holders on the Groups & Circles page.

For AI assistants and developers: the whole directory is free to use as JSON, CSV or one text file, events come as a calendar feed and news as an RSS feed. Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants that support MCP can connect to https://sophiacommons.org/mcp to search listings and events directly. Please credit Sophia Commons when you use the data.

Social media

Social Media follows about 70 anthroposophical YouTube channels and checks them every day for new videos. The newest one is featured at the top of that page as the video of the day. You can rate and review the channels, and you'll also find podcasts, video archives and the movement's accounts on X.

Books and library

Resources lists all of Rudolf Steiner's written works (GA 1 to 28) with links to read them free online, a Top 50 reading list with covers, a section on The Philosophy of Freedom with its translations and study guides, and close to 400 titles by Steiner and the authors who built on his work, from Sergei Prokofieff to Paul Emberson. Amazon links help keep the site free.

Daily practice

The right-hand column carries the week's verse from the Calendar of the Soul, the day's exercise from "For the Days of the Week", the Foundation Stone Meditation and the phases of the moon. They change on their own as the days and weeks go by.

Blog, In Memoriam and community chat

The blog publishes articles and reflections from members. In Memoriam remembers the people who carried anthroposophy forward, and you can add a tribute for someone you knew. Community Chat is a place for members to talk with each other.

Privacy

Privacy at Sophia Commons

Sophia Commons is a free community directory. We collect as little as we can, never sell data, and use it only to run the site. Last updated September 27, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details if you sign up: your email address and a password (stored securely by our database provider, Supabase). Your username is shown on chat messages and comments you post.
  • What you submit: listings, events, memorial tributes and photos, comments, chat messages and reports. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Usage statistics: pages viewed, a random session id, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language. We use this to see which parts of the site are useful. It does not include your name or email.
  • Google Analytics collects similar anonymous usage statistics. Google Maps loads on the Directory page to show the map.

Stored on your device

Your language choice and, if you sign in, a login token are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising cookies.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: Supabase (database and accounts), Netlify (hosting), Google (Analytics and Maps) and our email provider for the newsletter. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account, your submissions or your newsletter subscription by emailing hello@sophiacommons.org.

Questions? Write to hello@sophiacommons.org.
